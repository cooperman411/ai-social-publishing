# Optional Add-Ons

Add-ons are in the appendix. They are not the core path. Add one only when you ask for it, and only after the Muse first, Meta first core works.

The core rules do not change with an add-on. You approve the exact version first. Your own photos and video come first. Muse posts only to a verified account.

Read the full appendix on the repo home page: [APPENDIX.md](../../../APPENDIX.md). It explains each add-on in plain words, so you do not need to open the folders below to decide.

| Add-on | What it adds | When to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [LinkedIn](optional/linkedin.md) | A version of an approved post for LinkedIn, personal or company Page | You want the same story on LinkedIn, in addition to Meta |
| [TikTok Reels](optional/tiktok-reels.md) | Reuse of an approved vertical video on TikTok | You already have vertical video, or you want to add Reels, and you want TikTok as well |
| [Composio](optional/composio.md) | A third-party connection when Muse cannot reach an action directly | A needed action is missing in Muse, and Composio covers it exactly |
| [Buffer](optional/buffer.md) | A managed publishing queue | You want a queue instead of direct posting, and you accept its plan limits |
| [Adapting to another AI](optional/adapting-to-other-ai.md) | Advice for reading this skill in another AI, with no guarantees | You want to try the skill outside Muse, and you understand it is untested there |
| Manual publishing | Approved copy and files for you to post by hand | A tool cannot handle the format, or you want to post directly |

For each add-on, verify the account and the available actions with a read-only check before drafting for it. Confirm media support, scheduling or immediate publication, replies if needed, and how the tool returns status. Check current pricing and limits at the time of use. Keep setup separate from permission to post.

Other channels, for example X or YouTube, can be added the same way: as an add-on, with a verified account, an approved version adapted to that channel, and a recorded result. Do not add a channel to the core without a tested Muse path for it.
