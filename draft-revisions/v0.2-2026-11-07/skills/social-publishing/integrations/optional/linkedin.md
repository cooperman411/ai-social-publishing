# Optional Add-On: LinkedIn

This is an optional add-on. It is not the core path. Add it only when you ask for LinkedIn, and only after the Muse first, Meta first core works.

## What it adds

LinkedIn is a site for work and business contacts. This add-on posts a version of an approved Meta post to LinkedIn. It can go to your personal profile or to a company Page you administer.

It is a separate version, with its own approval. It is not a copy of the Facebook or Instagram caption.

## Set it up

1. Confirm whether the post is personal or company, and confirm the exact LinkedIn profile or Page. You must administer the Page to post as the Page.
2. Check how Muse will publish it. Use the browser route to LinkedIn when Muse has no direct LinkedIn connection. Carry the exact approved text, media files, and publication time into that task.
3. Verify the account and the available actions with a read-only check before drafting for LinkedIn. An account appearing in a list does not prove permission to publish.

## Adapt the copy

- Write for a work audience. Lead with the point, use one concrete detail, and keep it plain.
- Keep the same fact and the same credit as the approved Meta version. If the LinkedIn version changes a claim, a link, or a photo, it needs fresh approval.
- Use a link in the post only when you want it there. Do not add hashtags you did not ask for.
- For a company Page, use the company voice from the brief. For a personal profile, use your voice.

## Publish and record

- Submit only the approved LinkedIn version to its verified account.
- Record the outcome right away, per account. A scheduled item stays scheduled until LinkedIn verifies it is live. A live post needs a live link or post ID.
- If part of a batch fails on LinkedIn, record that result separately. Do not retry without checking LinkedIn first, and do not repost a version that already went live.
