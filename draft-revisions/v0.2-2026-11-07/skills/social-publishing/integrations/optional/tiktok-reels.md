# Optional Add-On: TikTok Reels

This is an optional add-on. It is not the core path. Add it only when you want Reels or video, and you want TikTok as well as Meta.

## Why this one is easy to add

TikTok works well with Meta for this use. You already make one clean vertical video for Instagram and Facebook Reels. That same file can be reused for TikTok, with a TikTok caption and its own approval. No new shoot is needed to add TikTok, and no new story is needed.

Use this add-on only if you want to do Reels or video at all. If you want photos only, skip it.

## What it adds

Reuse of an approved vertical video on TikTok. It is a separate account, with its own version and its own approval. It is not an automatic copy of the Instagram Reel.

## Set it up

1. Confirm you want video. Ask whether you have vertical video already, or want to start with photos and add video later.
2. Confirm the exact TikTok account. Verify the account and the available actions with a read-only check before drafting for it.
3. Check how Muse will publish it. Use the browser route to TikTok Studio when Muse has no direct TikTok connection. Use the browser route to TikTok Studio when Muse has no direct TikTok connection. Carry the exact approved video file, caption, and publication time into that task.
4. Check current TikTok format limits, music rules, and scheduling options at the time of use. Do not assume a music track cleared for Instagram is cleared for TikTok.

## Adapt the video and caption

- Use one clean master file: vertical 9:16, MP4, no watermark. That file fits Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, and TikTok.
- Keep on-screen text short, and keep the caption plain. Use the same fact and the same credit as the approved Meta version.
- If the TikTok caption, music, or video changes, it needs fresh approval.
- Do not post a TikTok version with music, text, or a claim that was not in the approved version.

## Publish and record

- Submit only the approved TikTok version to its verified account.
- Record the outcome right away, per account. A scheduled item stays scheduled until TikTok verifies it is live. A live post needs a live link or video ID.
- If part of a batch fails on TikTok, record that result separately. Do not retry without checking TikTok first, and do not repost a version that already went live.
