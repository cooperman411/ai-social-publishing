# Muse First

This is the core path. Use it first, and use it on its own until it works.

## What it is

Muse is the assistant. Muse connects directly to Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts, and Threads.

Muse drafts the batch. You approve the exact version. Muse schedules or publishes it, and Muse records the result.

No other tool, queue, or service is needed for the core path.

## Set it up

Do these steps in order.

1. In Muse, connect the Facebook Page, Instagram professional account, and Threads account you want to use. Use Muse's connection screen and sign in as the account owner.
2. For each account, verify the identity and the available actions with a read-only check. Check whether it can upload the chosen media, post now, schedule for a set time, support a reply chain, and confirm the result.
3. An account appearing in a list does not prove permission to publish. Confirm the capability from the actual tool and account.
4. Keep setup separate from permission to post. Connecting an account is not approval to publish.

## Use it

- Draft only for a verified account.
- Show the exact copy, media, credit, account, and time for approval, per account.
- Submit only the approved version.
- Record the outcome right away, per account. A scheduled item stays scheduled until the provider verifies it is live. A live post needs a live link or post ID.

## When it does not fit

If Muse cannot reach an account, or you want a channel outside the core, do not force the core path. Use an optional add-on from [APPENDIX.md](../../../APPENDIX.md) on the repo home page, or give yourself a manual handoff. Ask for the live link or other confirmation afterward.

Explain the tradeoff that matters for this run, including setup, current cost or limits, and any missing actions. Follow your preference when it fits the required actions.
