# Optional Publishing Routes

Start with the [Muse account guide](skills/social-publishing/integrations/muse-first.md). Use this page when you want another destination or need an action your Muse account can’t perform. Each route needs a verified account and approval of the exact post and time.

| Route | When to Use It |
| --- | --- |
| [Manual Publishing](skills/social-publishing/integrations/muse-first.md#post-it-yourself) | You want to post directly or Muse lacks an action for an account. |
| [LinkedIn](skills/social-publishing/integrations/optional/linkedin.md) | You want to adapt a story for your profile or a company Page. |
| [TikTok](skills/social-publishing/integrations/optional/tiktok-reels.md) | You have a vertical video you want to reuse. Check the music rights for TikTok. |
| [Composio](skills/social-publishing/integrations/optional/composio.md) | A toolkit covers an action you need and your Muse setup can use it. |
| [Buffer](skills/social-publishing/integrations/optional/buffer.md) | You want one place to review and schedule posts across connected accounts. |
| [Another Assistant](skills/social-publishing/integrations/optional/adapting-to-other-ai.md) | You want to reuse the brief, editorial steps, or review format in another assistant. |
