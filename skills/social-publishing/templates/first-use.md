# Set Up a Publishing Brief

Use what the operator has already shared. Ask only for the details needed for this run. Save the answers privately if the operator wants to reuse them.

1. Ask what the organization does, who should read the posts, and what the posts should accomplish.
2. Confirm the topics, geographic focus, and source preferences. Ask which company facts, links, or offers the assistant can use.
3. Ask for a voice guide, sample posts, or a few words describing the preferred tone. Use the default in [Editorial](../references/editorial.md) when the operator has no preference.
4. Confirm the channels, intended accounts, and person who approves the posts.
5. Agree on a proposed schedule. Capture the date or cadence, time zone, preferred times, review deadline, and any dates to avoid. Ask whether the operator wants scheduled publishing, a manual posting list, or drafts.
6. Confirm the image policy. Start with public domain or CC0 assets. Ask whether company images or other licenses are also acceptable.
7. Confirm where to keep the brief, calendar, files, and post history. Start with one draft when the operator wants to test the process.
