# Adapt a Post for LinkedIn

1. Choose your profile or a company Page you can post to. Ask Muse to confirm the account and the actions it can take.
2. Adapt the story for a work audience. Keep the facts and credit. Use the voice of the person or company that will publish it.
3. Review the exact LinkedIn copy, media, account, and time. Approve that version before posting.
4. Use the available publishing route or post it yourself. Record the LinkedIn result separately and add the live link when it’s available.
