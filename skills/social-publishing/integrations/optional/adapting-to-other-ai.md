# Reuse the Playbook With Another Assistant

Give your assistant the brief, editorial steps, and review format. Start with one draft and check the sources, media rights, and proposed time. You can publish it yourself and give the assistant the live link for its record.

If you want the assistant to publish, check the exact account and publishing action in that assistant first. Verify how it handles media, scheduled times, approval, and confirmation. Review each version before submitting it.
