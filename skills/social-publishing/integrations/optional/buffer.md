# Schedule Through Buffer

Use Buffer if you want a shared publishing queue and it supports the accounts and formats in your brief. Check the current channel limits and whether each channel can publish automatically or sends a reminder to post manually. [Buffer plans](https://buffer.com/pricing)

1. Connect the accounts you plan to use in Buffer. Follow the [assistant setup guide](https://support.buffer.com/articles/connecting-buffer-to-automation-tools-and-ai-assistants-MMoXpjDEEo) if your Muse setup supports the connection.
2. Ask Muse to confirm each account and the actions you need, including the media format and scheduling time.
3. Approve the copy, media, account, and time before it goes into the queue. Check the scheduled result in Buffer and record the live link once the post goes out.
