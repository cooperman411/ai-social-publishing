# Set Up Muse for Publishing

This is the core path. Use it first, and use it on its own until it works.

Muse is the assistant. Muse connects directly to Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts, and Threads. Muse drafts the batch. You approve the exact version. Muse schedules or publishes it, and Muse records the result. No other tool, queue, or service is needed for the core path.

## Set it up

Do these steps in order.

1. In Muse, connect the Facebook Page, Instagram professional account, and Threads account you want to use. Use Muse's connection screen and sign in as the account owner.
2. For each account, verify the identity and the available actions with a read-only check. Check whether it can upload the chosen media, post now, schedule for a set time, support a reply chain, and confirm the result.
3. An account appearing in a list does not prove permission to publish. Confirm the capability from the actual tool and account.
4. Keep setup separate from permission to post. Connecting an account is not approval to publish.

## Use it

- Draft only for a verified account.
- Show the exact copy, media, credit, account, and time for approval, per account.
- Submit only the approved version.
- Record the outcome right away, per account. A scheduled item stays scheduled until the provider verifies it is live. A live post needs a live link or post ID.

## Limits to know

- Facebook Page: Muse can publish, schedule, reschedule, and cancel. Scheduling is 10 minutes to 29 days ahead.
- Instagram: Muse can publish or schedule feed posts, Reels, carousels, and stories. Muse cannot list, edit, or cancel a scheduled Instagram post. Use the Instagram app for that.
- Threads: Muse publishes now. The Threads connector has no scheduling action, so a future Threads post needs a Muse task that runs at that time, or a draft plus manual posting. Threads needs fresh approval for each publish.
- Facebook personal: Muse posts text only. A personal photo or video needs the browser route on facebook.com, or manual posting with the approved copy and file.

## When it does not fit

If Muse cannot reach an account, keep that version in draft and explain what is missing. Use the approved copy and media to post it yourself, then give Muse the live link so Muse can record it.
